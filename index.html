Unhooded

Desktop devtools for your phone's browser.

Free and open source. Works on any site, even locked-down ones. 18 KB, zero tracking.

  • ConsoleLogs, errors and a JS prompt.
  • ElementsTap to inspect, live-edit CSS.
  • NetworkRequests, status, timing, bodies.
  • StorageLocal, session and cookies.

30-second install

  1. Bookmark this pageUse your browser's share or ⋮ menu.
  2. Copy the code
  3. Swap the addressEdit the bookmark you just made, delete its address and paste the code in its place.

Usage

  1. Open any siteGo to the page you want to inspect.
  2. Open your bookmarksTap Unhooded in your bookmarks.
